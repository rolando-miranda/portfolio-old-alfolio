## Context

From 2021 to 2025 I worked on Class III medical device manufacturing at MicroVention–Terumo and then Terumo Neuroscience, primarily on microcatheter product lines. The work spanned process validation, raw-material qualification, yield improvement, and cross-functional coordination across R&D, QA, Manufacturing, and Regulatory.

The work itself is proprietary. The summary below describes outcomes and the type of engineering involved, not specific processes, equipment, or product details.

## Selected outcomes

**Doubled production capacity on highest-volume microcatheter line.** Managed a cross-functional validation project that coordinated R&D, QA, Manufacturing, and Regulatory through qualification protocol development and execution. The expanded capacity addressed sustained demand that had previously been capacity-limited.

**$3M backorder reduction through raw-material validation strategy.** Executed a systematic raw-material qualification approach across five product lines. The work bridged supplier qualification, process re-validation, and inventory planning, and resolved a multi-line backorder situation through engineering rather than purchasing.

**Yield improvements across assigned product lines.** Exceeded corporate yield targets by standardizing SOPs and aligning production, quality, and engineering teams on process improvements. The work emphasized changes that were durable — reproducible across operators and shifts — rather than one-off optimizations.

## Engineering practice

GxP-compliant process validation, IQ/OQ/PQ protocol development, FMEA, root-cause analysis, statistical process characterization (Minitab), and Lean/Six Sigma methodology applied to a regulated production environment. Reference letters from former managers available on request.
