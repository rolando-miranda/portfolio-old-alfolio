## Overview

Capstone project for my BSc. in Mechanical Engineering (Universidad de Costa Rica). The goal was a working desktop tribometer capable of running both pin-on-disk and block-on-ring wear tests with switchable fixtures and instrumented load and friction measurement.

## What it covered

- **Mechanical design.** Frame, drive assembly, and interchangeable test fixtures designed for stiffness and alignment. Solid-mechanics analysis on critical load paths.
- **FEA.** Finite-element analysis on the main load-bearing components to confirm deflection budgets and identify stress concentrations.
- **Mechatronic instrumentation.** Sensor selection and integration for normal force, friction force, and rotational speed; data acquisition strategy.
- **Materials & wear testing background.** Standard pin-on-disk and block-on-ring test protocols informed the spec for sample geometry, load range, and sliding speed.

## What I took from it

The project was my first end-to-end mechanical design with a real integration burden: every subsystem had constraints the others didn't anticipate. Lessons learned here — particularly around fixture stiffness and sensor placement — directly informed how I approached process equipment design at Terumo and the lab instrumentation I now build at NYU.
