I'm a mechanical engineer with four years of industry experience in Class III medical device manufacturing, now pursuing an MS in Mechanical Engineering at [NYU Tandon](https://engineering.nyu.edu/) (graduating May 2027). My coursework focuses on **mechatronics, linear control, thermal engineering, and autonomous systems**.

Before NYU, I worked at **Terumo Neuroscience** as a Manufacturing Engineer II, where I led validation efforts across multiple microcatheter product lines — including a project that doubled production capacity for our highest-volume device and a raw-material validation strategy that reduced $3M in backorders. I hold a Master's in Project Management (*Suma Cum Laude*, ULACIT 2025) and a Bachelor's with Honors in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Costa Rica.

I'm currently a **Graduate Course Assistant in the Thermo-fluids Laboratory** at NYU Tandon, where I design and build instrumentation for student experiments and develop laboratory protocols. Alongside coursework I'm building a peristaltic pump with open-loop flow control for my mechatronics class, and have previously developed autonomous planning and control algorithms for a simulated Mars rover.

My interests sit at the intersection of **mechatronic design, thermal systems, and medical device engineering** — areas where careful instrumentation, control, and process discipline all matter. I'm open to conversations about R&D roles, research collaborations, and consulting opportunities in these areas.
